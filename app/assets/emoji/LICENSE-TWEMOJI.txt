Twemoji emoji artwork — attribution and licence
===============================================

The PNG files in this directory are from Twemoji (https://twemoji.twitter.com/),
release 15.0.3, fetched from the jdecked/twemoji mirror on jsDelivr by
tools/fetch_twemoji.py. They are bundled here so the renderer never downloads
anything at runtime.

Modifications: none — the 72x72 PNGs are stored byte-for-byte as published.
The emoji *choices* (which emoji, which emotion, which keywords) live in
whitelist.json, which is Clipmake's own data, not part of Twemoji.
